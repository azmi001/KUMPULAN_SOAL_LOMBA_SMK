From Brazil to LyonJoin us as we weave a vibrant tapestry of culture, craftsmanship, and colour at the Silk in Lyon event, within the historic walls of the Palais de la Bourse in the heart of Lyon.This year, Silk in Lyon 2024 celebrates Brazil as our country of honour, blending Brazilian vibrancy with silk elegance. The theme, “From Brazil to Lyon,” showcases the rich cultural exchange and the luxurious essence of silk, embodying innovation, beauty, and tradition in a vibrant homage to Brazil’s spirited palette.
